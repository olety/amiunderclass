A 1950s socialist-realist propaganda poster silkscreened in four flat inks: warm paper, deep cerulean, apricot, and one coral. A huge radiant sun fills the top half, its rays drawn as long smooth tapering tentacles that curl gently at the tips. Beneath it a small group of heroic adults stand on a raised platform bathed in apricot light, faces lifted, one of them a woman holding a folder. Below the platform a vast orderly crowd of identical small figures in cerulean silhouette fills the shade down to the bottom edge. A blank paper band runs across the bottom fifth, ready for a slogan. Bold, optimistic, monumental composition, visible screen-print grain.
