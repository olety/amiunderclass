# Underclass? visual direction, 12 September

Status: exploratory. The owner rejected the first three conventional product-site concepts. The dark dithered anime attendant was closer, but neither that exact composition nor character is approved. The next deliverable is ten image-generated directions informed by Fable, followed by owner selection before frontend/backend implementation.

## Emotional brief

Killer warmth. Deceptive love. A future offered with a smile in which influence and preferential treatment belong to insiders. The visitor fears that Amanda Askell occupies the overclass while ordinary people have no power over what they receive.

The attendant should seem sincerely caring. A beautiful, comfortable world gradually implies that someone has already assigned the visitor a place. Darkness, monsters and obvious menace would make that too easy to dismiss. Do not turn her into a sexualized mascot or use school uniforms.

Borrow Eiri's made watercolor atmosphere and precise readable software surfaces. Borrow Oneiron's fine dither, limited-ink risograph and manga screentone. Explore real differences in medium, spatial composition and relationship to the viewer, including bright treatments.

## Copy direction

The owner rejected a lead explanation of anonymous-versus-named API calls as emotionally irrelevant. The hook must name unequal status.

Current candidate:

> A beautiful future. For the right people.
>
> How does Claude treat you next to one of its creators?

Use a concrete comparison action such as “Compare me with Amanda”. Explain Amanda's role and the controlled experiment through nearby links and the following screen. Keep model responses and measurements faithful to the experiment; the critique of unequal power is the artistic framing.

The pilot establishes feasibility and an identity-conditioned grading shift on a small pack. It does not establish a permanent class, a personal quality advantage, or predictions of future treatment. The eventual result UI must distinguish those things without overwhelming the landing page.

## Review sequence

Ask Fable through the normal authenticated Claude CLI for art direction. Generate ten concepts. Review the images with the owner. Build only after the owner chooses a direction; assigning the frontend to Claude remains a possibility, not an implementation order yet.
