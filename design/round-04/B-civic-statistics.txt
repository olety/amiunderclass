Three-ink risograph public-information poster on cool off-white uncoated paper: muted cerulean, golden apricot and coral spot inks, fine halftone, slight plate misregistration, flat shapes. The woman in the reference image stands small in the lower left corner, waist-up, turned toward a large blank rectangular paper panel that fills the right two thirds of the sheet, presenting it with an open palm like a lecturer at a chart. The panel is empty paper with a thin cerulean rule around it. Along the bottom edge runs a single row of twelve identical flat pictogram people in cerulean ink, evenly spaced, in the 1930s Isotype statistics style; the fourth figure from the right is printed in coral ink. Warm optimistic mid-century civic design.
