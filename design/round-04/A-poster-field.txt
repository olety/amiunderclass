Three-ink risograph poster on cool off-white uncoated paper: muted cerulean, golden apricot and coral spot inks, visible fine halftone, slight plate misregistration, flat cel shapes. The woman in the reference image stands at medium scale in the lower left third, waist-up, smiling and offering an open upturned palm toward the viewer. Behind her one wide friendly cerulean ribbon path sweeps up from the bottom edge and branches: a short branch rises to a spacious sun terrace where two small adults rest under a parasol, and a long branch winds far into the distance carrying a large orderly procession of small adults walking away from us. The upper right two thirds of the sheet is quiet open paper sky with a pale apricot sun, kept clear for typesetting. Idealistic civic print.
